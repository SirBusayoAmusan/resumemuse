Questions

Good things to know

Is it really free?
Yes. ResumeMuse works on a bring-your-own-key model. You connect your own free API key from Groq once, and it stays in your browser. There is no subscription and no card required to build and export a resume.
Does it invent things to make me look better?
No, and this is the whole point of the product. ResumeMuse never fabricates a number, job title, employer, date or metric. It rewrites what you actually did into stronger language, and if a real figure would make a bullet better, it asks you for it rather than inventing one. That is the version that holds up in an interview.
Will my resume actually pass ATS filters?
ATS compliance is mostly about structure, not decoration. ResumeMuse uses single-column, parsing-safe layouts with standard headings and clean text, and shows you a live match score against the specific job so you can close the gaps before you apply.
What do I need to get started?
A job description and your experience. You can upload an existing resume to use as context, or start from scratch and answer a few focused questions. Either way, the job post comes first, because that is what everything gets tailored against.
Is my data stored anywhere?
No. Your answers and your key stay in your browser, and files you upload are read locally. Nothing is saved on our servers, so closing the tab clears it. You are always in control of your own information.
Does it do more than a resume?
Yes. From the same job post and the answers you have already given, ResumeMuse also drafts a matching cover letter and generates likely interview questions, so the three pieces stay consistent with each other.
