Honest by design

We won't invent achievements.

If you managed a team of five, we won't say ten. If you don't know the exact revenue number, we won't make one up. We'll ask, and if you don't know, the line stays honest without it.

WHAT WE WON'T DO

"Increased revenue by 47% and led a team of 12 across three markets."

WHAT WE'LL DO INSTEAD

"Grew revenue across three markets while leading the partnerships team." Then ask if you can put a number to it.

The best resume is still an honest one. It just needs to be a well aimed one too.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do I need an existing resume?

No. You can start from nothing and answer questions instead, or upload what you have and skip anything it already covers. Both paths end in a complete resume.

Will it lie to make me look better?

No. If a bullet needs a number and you haven't given one, it asks you for an estimate. If you skip it, the line stays qualitative rather than getting an invented statistic attached to it. That rule is enforced before anything reaches your resume, not just requested politely.

What is the match score for?

To show you what's missing before you hit Apply, not to grade you. It tells you which requirements you've covered, which the employer keeps mentioning, and where a bullet is weaker than it needs to be. You can export at any score.

Will my resume get past the screening software?

Every template is single column with standard section headings and no information hidden in icons or headers, which is where most resumes get misread. Structure is the part that matters, and it's the part we control.

How long does one application take?

A few minutes. Most of that is answering questions about work you already did. Reusing the same answers for the cover letter and interview prep is what saves the real time.

Is what I paste kept private?

The preview above runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. In the full product your job descriptions and answers are used to build your documents and nothing else.
